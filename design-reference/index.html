INTELLO ANSWER · 4 SOURCES

Your authentication architecture, explained.

Your platform uses an OIDC-based identity layer to authenticate users, while a separate authorization service manages organization, workspace, and document-level permissions.

Requests are scoped to the user's active organization and workspace. Postgres enforces row-level restrictions, while the knowledge retrieval layer performs additional source-level checks before any evidence is sent to the language model.

These safeguards protect against cross-tenant data leakage and ensure that answers cite the correct source version.
