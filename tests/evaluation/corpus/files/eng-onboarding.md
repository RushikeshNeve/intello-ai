# Engineering onboarding

Welcome to the engineering team. This guide covers your first two weeks.

## Accounts and equipment

New engineers receive a laptop on day one. Standard issue is a 14-inch notebook with 32 GB of memory. Single sign-on is activated by the IT desk before your start date, and hardware security keys are handed out at the welcome session.

## First week

Your onboarding buddy walks you through the repository layout and the local development stack. You are expected to merge a first small change by the end of the first week. Mentor check-ins happen every Thursday at 10:00 during the first month.

## Access requests

Production access is not granted during onboarding. After 90 days and a completed security training, you may request read-only production access from your team lead.
