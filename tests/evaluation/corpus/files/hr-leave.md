# Leave policy

Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year and must be taken before 31 March.

## Sick leave

Employees receive up to 10 days of fully paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required from the third consecutive day of absence.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks.

## Requests

Leave requests are submitted in the HR portal at least 14 days in advance for absences longer than five days.
