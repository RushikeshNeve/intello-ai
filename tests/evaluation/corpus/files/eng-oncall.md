# On-call policy

Engineers join the on-call rotation after completing three months in the team. The rotation is weekly and hands over every Monday at 09:00 UTC.

## Response times

The primary on-call engineer must acknowledge a page within 15 minutes. If there is no acknowledgement after 30 minutes the page escalates to the secondary engineer, and after 45 minutes to the engineering manager.

## Compensation

On-call weeks are compensated with a flat allowance of 350 EUR. Every hour spent on an incident outside working hours earns one hour of time off in lieu.

## Handover

The outgoing engineer writes a handover note that lists open incidents and flaky alerts.
